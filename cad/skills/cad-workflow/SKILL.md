# CAD design

Record units, interfaces and tolerances before editing geometry. Preserve editable .FCStd and .scad sources. Export STL or STEP as derived artifacts and inspect the geometry as well as command exit status. Distinguish geometry validation from physical fit that requires measurement or fabrication.

Keep generated outputs under build/ or exports/. Report which checks ran and what remains unverified.
