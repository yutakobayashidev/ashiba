# 3D printing

Identify the printer, nozzle, material and calibrated profile before slicing. Preserve the 3MF project and slicer settings. Inspect orientation, supports and toolpaths, and export G-code for review. Distinguish slicing checks from physical print quality. Do not start a printer merely by entering the shell or generating an artifact.

Keep generated outputs under build/ or exports/. Report which checks ran and what remains unverified.
